# Landing page assets

All demo names, relationships and stories are fictional. The family is inspired by
The Simpsons and is not affiliated with its rights holders.

## Portraits and photographs

Every portrait in `people/` and every image in `media/` is an original SVG
approximation drawn for this landing page. They are not official artwork and are
not copied from any screenshot or promotional image. No third-party assets are
downloaded at build or runtime, so there is nothing to regenerate.

The `media/thumbs` copies are the same SVGs as `media/full`; the graph simply asks
for the smaller path so the demo data resembles real uploaded files.

## Demo family note

The Simpsons names and relationships belong to their rights holders. This tree is a
simplified three-generation slice (the Bouvier grandparents, Homer and Marge, and
their children) chosen to keep the graph readable, not an attempt at canon.
